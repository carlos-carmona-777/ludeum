Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version: Ludéum has no ads and no trackers, and it does not sell or share your data. Your progress stays on your device and in your own account (iCloud on iPhone and iPad; Google on Android). On iPhone and iPad, online play goes through Game Center, and the little we store goes to the app’s public iCloud database (Apple’s CloudKit): anonymous statistics about games against the characters, which you can turn off, and the open tables waiting for a rival. On Android, online play uses our Firebase project (a Google service) and stores only what is needed to play. Everything is explained below.

Who is responsible

Ludéum is an app by Carlos Carmona (Tzinitzcan). If you have any questions about this policy, write to us from the support page.

If you write to us through the contact form, we use your name, email and message only to reply to you; we don’t share them or use them for anything else.

Data the app saves

On your device and in your iCloud:

  • Your progress: statistics, the record of games played, challenges, stamps, tournament laurels, achievements earned, tutorials completed and your online rank.
  • Your profile name and emblem.

Only on your device:

  • Games in progress.
  • Your settings: board style, background, appearance, sound, music, pieces and dice.

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress is also stored in your iCloud account, in the app’s own space, so it is the same on all your devices and is not lost if you delete the app or change iPhones. Apple keeps that copy with your account; we have no access to it. Without iCloud, everything stays only on your device. You can erase your statistics from your profile (Erase Stats): they are also erased on your other devices and in iCloud.

Anonymous statistics

On iPhone and iPad, every game you finish (or abandon) against a character sends an anonymous record to the app’s public iCloud database (CloudKit, an Apple service). It tells us whether a character turned out too easy or too hard. The record contains:

  • the game, the variant, the difficulty and the result (win, loss, draw or abandoned);
  • how long it lasted, how many times you had played that character before, and game details such as your side or the number of hands;
  • the app version and whether it is an iPhone or an iPad.

It does not contain your name, your nickname, your email or your account. CloudKit only adds an internal ID for whoever created the record, which Apple generates for this app and which does not tell us who you are; we do not link it to anything. Online games, two-player games on one device and tutorials are not counted. Without iCloud nothing is sent: records wait on your device.

They are on by default and you can turn them off at any time in Profile › Preferences › Share Statistics; turning them off also deletes any records not yet sent. On Android they are not sent yet.

Game Center

Game Center is an Apple service and is optional: without it you can play everything except online. If you sign in, the app receives your nickname and a player identifier from Game Center, and sends Apple:

  • your progress on achievements;
  • your points for each week in the tournaments. The leaderboards are public on Game Center: other players see your nickname, your points and your position.

We do not receive your email, your photo or any other data from your Apple Account. Game Center is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Online play

Online games, live or turn-based, go through Game Center. Only what is needed to play is sent to your rival: the moves, the game settings, your online rank, your emblem and whether you are a Patron (to show your frame). Your rival sees your Game Center nickname. There is no chat or messaging, and your profile name is not sent. The games themselves are not kept outside Game Center.

Open tables. If you look for a turn-based rival and nobody is waiting, your game is announced as an open table in the app’s public iCloud database (CloudKit) so another player can sit down. Any Ludéum player can see your Game Center nickname, your emblem, your online rank, the game, its rules and when you opened it. The table is deleted when someone sits down, when you cancel it, or after 7 days without a rival. So that two players cannot sit at once, whoever sits leaves a marker with the table’s name and no personal data. Without iCloud no tables are opened.

Purchases

Patron is a one-time in-app purchase. It is processed by Apple with your App Store account: we do not receive your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether the purchase is active (also if it reaches you through Family Sharing or when you restore purchases).

Android

The Android version saves your progress just like the iPhone one: on your phone and, if Google backup is turned on, in your Google account. Unfinished games stay on the phone only.

Google Play Games is optional: without it you can play everything except online. If you sign in, your progress is also kept in Play Games Saved Games on your account, and Google receives your achievement progress and your weekly tournament points. Leaderboards are public: other players see your player name, your points and your rank. Play Games is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Online play. On Android, online games go through our Firebase project, a Google service:

  • When you play online, Firebase gives you a user ID linked to your Play Games account. We do not receive your email or your photo.
  • Each game stores the moves, the settings, your Play Games player name, your emblem, your online level and whether you are a Patron. Only the two players can read it. There is no chat and there are no messages. Finished games are deleted after 30 days and invitations expire after 30 minutes.
  • Open tables. If you look for a turn-based rival and nobody is waiting, your game is announced as an open table: other Ludéum players online see your Play Games player name, your emblem, whether you are a Patron, the game and its rules. It is deleted when someone sits down, when you cancel it, or after 7 days.
  • If you allow notifications, we store a notification ID for your phone so we can tell you when it is your turn. You can turn them off at any time in Android settings.
  • Firebase App Check, with Google Play Integrity, checks that requests come from the genuine app, to prevent abuse.

This data is encrypted in transit and used only to make online play work: we do not sell or share it. Patron purchases on Android are processed by Google Play with your account: we do not receive your payment details.

What Ludéum does not use

There is no advertising, no advertising identifier, no cross-app tracking, no third-party analytics and no third-party crash reports. The app does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or notifications. The historical photos come inside the app. The only things the app downloads are announcements, cover images and difficulty settings from our website, without sending any of your data.

On Android, the app may ask for permission to show notifications (to tell you about your online games); it is optional.

Children

Ludéum does not ask anyone for personal data, including minors, and it is not directed at children under 13. Game Center features can be restricted with Apple's Screen Time, and Play Games features with Google Family Link.

Your rights

What the app saves is on your device and in your iCloud account, and you control it. The anonymous statistics contain nothing that tells us who you are, so we cannot find yours to hand over or delete; you can stop sending them at any time. Your open tables are deleted when you cancel them, or automatically after 7 days. What Game Center stores with your account (achievements and leaderboards) is managed by Apple from your Apple Account.

On Android, what online play stores in Firebase is deleted automatically 30 days after the game ends. If you want us to delete it sooner, or to delete your ID and notification data, write to us from the support page and we will do it within 30 days. What Play Games keeps with your account is managed from your Google account.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with your data, we will update this page and the date above before releasing the change.
