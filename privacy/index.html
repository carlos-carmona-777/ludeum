Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

In short: Ludéum does not collect personal data, does not store it on its own servers, and does not share it. There are no accounts, analytics, trackers or ads.

Data that stays on your device

  • Your saved games, statistics, challenges, stamps, laurels and settings (board style, sound, music, appearance) are stored only on your iPhone or iPad.
  • You can erase your statistics from the profile screen in the app. Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

Game Center

If Game Center is turned on, Apple manages achievements, the weekly tournament leaderboards and online play with your account. Ludéum only uses what Game Center provides in order to play: your Game Center nickname and picture, which your opponent can see. We never receive your email or any other data from your Apple Account. All of this is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Online play

Online games, live or turn-based, go through Apple's Game Center servers. Only the moves and the state of the game are sent, and only to your opponent. Ludéum has no servers of its own and keeps no copy of those games outside Game Center.

Purchases

If the app offers in-app purchases, Apple processes them with your App Store account. Ludéum never receives your payment details; it only learns whether a purchase was made so it can unlock what you bought.

Children

Ludéum collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with your data, we will update this page and the date above before the change is released.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Write to us from the support page.
