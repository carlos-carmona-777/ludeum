Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The most important thing: Ludéum does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no trackers and no advertising. What the app saves stays on your iPhone or iPad; what passes through Game Center and the App Store is managed by Apple.

Who is responsible

Ludéum is an app by Carlos Carmona (Tzinitzcan). If you have any questions about this policy, write to us from the support page.

If you write to us through the contact form, we use your name, email and message only to reply to you; we don’t share them or use them for anything else.

Data that stays on your device

  • Your saved games, your statistics and the record of games played, challenges, stamps, tournament laurels and your online rank.
  • Your settings: board style, background, appearance, sound, music, pieces and dice.
  • Your profile name and emblem.

All of this is stored only on your device: it is not uploaded to servers of ours or synced with iCloud. You can erase your statistics from your profile (Erase Stats), and if you delete the app, everything it saved is deleted.

Game Center

Game Center is an Apple service and is optional: without it you can play everything except online. If you sign in, the app receives your nickname and a player identifier from Game Center, and sends Apple:

  • your progress on achievements;
  • your points for each week in the tournaments. The leaderboards are public on Game Center: other players see your nickname, your points and your position.

We do not receive your email, your photo or any other data from your Apple Account. Game Center is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Online play

Online games, live or turn-based, go through Game Center. Only what is needed to play is sent to your rival: the moves, the game settings, your online rank, your emblem and whether you are a Patron (to show your frame). Your rival sees your Game Center nickname. There is no chat or messaging, and your profile name is not sent. Ludéum does not keep online games outside Game Center.

Purchases

Patron is a one-time in-app purchase. It is processed by Apple with your App Store account: we do not receive your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether the purchase is active (also if it reaches you through Family Sharing or when you restore purchases).

What Ludéum does not use

There is no advertising, no advertising identifier, no cross-app tracking, no analytics and no third-party crash reports. The app does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or notifications. The historical photos come inside the app: nothing is downloaded from the internet.

Children

Ludéum does not collect data from anyone, including minors. Game Center features can be restricted with Apple's Screen Time.

Your rights

Since we do not keep any data about you, we have nothing to give you, correct or delete: everything is on your device and you control it. What Game Center stores with your account (achievements and leaderboards) is managed by Apple from your Apple Account.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with your data, we will update this page and the date above before releasing the change.
