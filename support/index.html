Support

Something not working, found a mistake in the rules, or have an idea? We'd love to hear from you.

Write to us: use the Ludéum contact form on the Tzinitzcan website (the form is in Spanish, but you can write in English). Leave your name, email and message, and we’ll get back to you.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play online?

Online play uses Game Center. Open Settings › Game Center on your iPhone or iPad and sign in with your Apple Account. Then, in the app, tap Online for a random rival or With Friends to invite with a link or a code. If nobody shows up within a few seconds, you can play one of the app's characters in the meantime.

What is the difference between “Live” and “Turn-Based”?

In Live games, both players play at the same time. In Turn-Based games, each player moves whenever they can and Game Center lets you know when it's your turn. Three Men's Morris, Conquián and Cribbage are Live only.

Why are the rules different from the ones I know?

For many ancient games the complete rules have not survived. Ludéum follows the reconstructions of historians and archaeologists, and when they disagree it lets you choose a variant. In each game, the “i” button explains what we know and what we don't.

How do I learn a new game?

On each game's page, tap Learn to Play: it's a guided tutorial at Easy difficulty. The “i” cards have the history, an animated explanation and the full rules.

When do the weekly tournaments start?

The Weekly League and the Game of the Week start on Monday, November 2, 2026. Each week runs from Monday at 12:00 AM to Sunday at 11:59 PM, Mexico City time. To appear on the leaderboards you need Game Center.

Do my statistics move to another device?

Your games, statistics and challenges are stored only on the device you play on. Game Center achievements and leaderboards do follow you to any device signed in with your account.

Do I have to pay to play?

No. All the games are free to play, with no ads. Patron is an optional one-time purchase to support the museum: it adds frames, emblems, pieces, dice and “Museum” boards.

I'm already a Patron, but it doesn't show up on another device

Open the Patron screen and tap Restore Purchases with the same App Store account. If someone in your family made the purchase, it is shared through Family Sharing.

How do I change the language?

Ludéum is available in Spanish, English, French, German, Portuguese, Catalan and Italian, and uses your device's language. To change it only for the app, open Settings › Ludéum › Language.

How do I change the board or turn on dark mode?

In the Games tab, tap the palette button at the top: there you choose the board style, the background and the appearance (System, Light or Dark). Next to it are the buttons to turn off sounds and music.
