Frequently asked questions

Is the app in English?

Not yet. Ludéum's interface, rules and history cards are currently in Spanish.

How do I play online?

Online play uses Game Center. Open Settings › Game Center on your iPhone or iPad and sign in with your Apple Account. Then pick a game in the app, tap En línea (Online) and find an opponent or invite a friend. If nobody shows up within a few seconds, you can play one of the app's rivals in the meantime.

What is the difference between live and turn-based?

In live games both players play at the same time. In turn-based games each player moves whenever they can, and Game Center lets you know when it's your turn. Three Men's Morris and Conquián are live only.

Why are the rules different from the ones I know?

For many ancient games the complete rules have not survived. Ludéum follows the reconstructions of historians and archaeologists, and when they disagree it lets you choose a variant. In each game, the "i" button explains what we know and what we don't.

Do my statistics move to another device?

Your games, statistics and challenges are stored only on the device you play on. Game Center achievements and leaderboards follow you to any device signed in with your account.

When do the weekly tournaments start?

The Weekly League and the Game of the Week start on Monday, November 2, 2026. Each week runs from Monday 0:00 to Sunday 23:59, Mexico City time.
